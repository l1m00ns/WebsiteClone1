Your contributions pay for hosting, the database and storage, and keep the site free of ads and monthly fees.

What donators get

  • Early accessEvery new upload is yours 48 hours before everyone else.
  • Booster-only sourcesAll the locked booster and donator downloads.
  • Dumper offsetsFull Roblox offsets from our own dumper, far more than the public set.
  • AI agent accessLet Claude Code, Cursor and others search and download sources for you.
  • Web proxyOpen any site with ads and redirects blocked, without showing your IP.
  • Tools64 browser utilities: formatters, encoders, converters and more.
  • Source requestsAsk for a source; donator requests are filled first.
  • Donator roleThe Donator role and badge, on the site and in Discord.
