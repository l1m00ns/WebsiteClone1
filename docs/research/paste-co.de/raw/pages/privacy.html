Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 April 2026

1. Overview

paste-co.de ("we", "our", "the site") is a project archive and community platform operated for educational purposes. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored, and your rights. By using the site you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please stop using the site.

2. Data We Collect

When you log in with Discord we receive, via OAuth 2.0, the following data directly from Discord:

  • Discord user ID, username, and display name
  • Avatar image URL (hosted on Discord's CDN)
  • Email address (used only for account identification, never displayed publicly)
  • Guild (server) membership and role information for the MacroStack server

This information is stored in our database and associated with activity you take on the site (favorites, forum posts, submissions).

When you browse anonymously your IP address is immediately hashed using HMAC-SHA256 with a server-side secret. The raw IP address is never stored. The resulting hash is truncated to 16 hex characters and stored in our activity and view logs solely for deduplication and abuse prevention purposes. The hash cannot be reversed to recover your IP address.

When you submit a project we store your Discord identity (see above), the submitted file URL and full project metadata, and your IP hash. Submissions go into an admin review queue before being published.

Usage data — we count project views and downloads per project. A 10-minute deduplication window is applied using your IP hash. No detailed browsing profiles are built.

Community feedback — source reputation votes, comments, quick comments, reports, anonymous display names, and logged-in Discord attribution may be stored so admins can moderate source quality and abuse reports.

4. Data Retention

Session cookies expire after 7 days. Activity logs (IP hashes) are kept for 90 days and then purged. Forum posts and submissions are retained for as long as the site operates or until you request deletion. Discord profile data (username, avatar) is refreshed on each login and removed if you request account deletion.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of your personal data. EEA/UK residents have these rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR. To exercise any right, contact us on Discord at discord.gg/macrostack or open a thread in the support channel with your Discord user ID. We will respond within 30 days.

You may delete your account data at any time by contacting us. Deleting your account removes stored favorites, submissions, and forum posts attributed to your Discord ID. IP hashes are anonymised by design and cannot be individually deleted.

6. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA / UK)

  • Legitimate interest — anonymous view/download counting and deduplication using irreversible IP hashes.
  • Contract performance — storing your Discord identity to provide favorites, forum participation, and submission attribution.
  • Consent — personalised advertising cookies (you can withdraw consent at any time via the cookie banner).
  • Legal obligation — retaining abuse/scan logs to detect and prevent malicious uploads.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
