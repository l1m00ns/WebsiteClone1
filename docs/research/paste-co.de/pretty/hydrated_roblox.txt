<h2 class="text-sm font-semibold text-foreground">What are Roblox offsets?</h2>
<p class="text-xs leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground">Offsets are memory addresses relative to the Roblox executable base, pointing to specific functions and structures. Researchers use them to study engine behavior and develop external tooling.</p>
</div>
<div class="space-y-1.5">
<h2 class="text-sm font-semibold text-foreground">How are they updated?</h2>
<p class="text-xs leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground">Roblox updates several times a week, shifting the memory layout each time. Offsets here are re-dumped shortly after each update.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="space-y-4">
<div class="space-y-1.5">
<h2 class="text-sm font-semibold text-foreground">Understanding FFlags</h2>
<p class="text-xs leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground">Fast Flags are runtime toggles Roblox engineers use to switch features on or off without a client update. Tracking them reveals upcoming features, performance changes and hidden diagnostics.</p>
</div>
<div class="space-y-1.5">
<h2 class="text-sm font-semibold text-foreground">Educational use</h2>
<p class="text-xs leading-relaxed text-muted-foreground">All data is provided for educational purposes, for people learning reverse engineering, memory management and platform security analysis.</p>
